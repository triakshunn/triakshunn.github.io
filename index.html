About Me

I'm currently a master's student at the University of Michigan's Robotics Institute, where I am advised by Prof. Dimitra Panagou. My research interests broadly encompass developing optimal control policies for robots to be able to safely and generally navigate our world. Previously, I worked as a research assistant at Singapore University of Technology and Design (SUTD) advised by Dr. Malika Meghjani, where I worked on multi-agent collaboration problems with human in the loop, and deployed these algorithms on real robots. I have also had the pleasure of working with Manas Gupta on designing sample-efficient architectural components for control tasks using bio-inspired methods like Hebbian Learning.

Projects

Zero-Shot Human-Robot Coordination in Multi-Agent Systems

Akshunn Trivedi, Malika Meghjani

Developed decentralized policy architectures for robots to coordinate with unseen human partners without prior calibration. Deployed and evaluated zero-shot cooperation algorithms on physical ground vehicles in multi-agent environments.

Preprint · Code

Bio-Inspired Hebbian Learning for Continuous Robot Control

Akshunn Trivedi, Manas Gupta

Investigated sample-efficient control architectures utilizing local Hebbian plasticity rules. Developed simulated agents capable of fast adaptation in continuous control environments, reducing dependency on global reward backpropagation.
